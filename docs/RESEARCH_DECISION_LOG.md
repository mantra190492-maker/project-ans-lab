# Project ANS — Research decision log

**Purpose:** Keep the primary scientific objective stable when agents, datasets or commercial advice change.

## D001 — 2026-11-15: Founder intent controls research direction

**Decision:** The primary mission is to **discover and validate a genuinely differentiated physiological marker of ANS dysfunction or functional deterioration**. The target is not biological age, mortality forecasting or any particular product niche. The goal is ambitious and currently unproven.

**Why:** We repeatedly let accessible datasets, exciting biomarker papers, and an external agent's diabetes-screening business critique redefine the mission. This was a research-management failure. The founder did **not** authorize those pivots.

**Rule:** Every proposal goes to one of three categories:
- **DISCOVERY:** Explicit, falsifiable, potentially novel relation between physiology and independently defined autonomic dysfunction / worsening.
- **SUPPORTING VALIDATION:** ECG/BP quality, BRS, HRF and signal-analysis work that could validate measurements but cannot be branded as a discovery.
- **SECONDARY:** General mortality, biological age, marketing, medical-device business scenarios. These may be recorded separately but may not substitute for DISCOVERY.

**Evidence:** [Locked charter](../config/research_charter.json) and [governance rules](RESEARCH_GUARDRAILS.md).

## D002 — 2026-11-15: Prior work is not new invention

**Decision:** HRV, BRS, HRF/PIP, existing orthostatic recovery and any simple weighted sum must not be called a novel biomarker. They remain measurement components or comparators until a precise, verified new scientific claim emerges.

**Evidence:** [Novelty audit](NOVELTY_RESET_2026-11-15.md), [agent comparison](AGENT_EVIDENCE_COMPARISON_BOARD.md).

## D003 — 2026-11-15: AFDV is unvalidated and should not be promoted

**Decision:** [AFDV-001](../research/proposals/AFDV-001.json) is an **IDEA**, with unknown novelty and no acquired longitudinal clinical ANS reference data. It cannot move to clinical validity until relevant gates pass. Repeating benign stand measurements is engineering, not evidence of deterioration.

## D004 — Current one-step priority

**Single active supporting milestone:** Resolve whether PRCP's public ECG/BP annotations and repeated posture challenges can yield reproducible, properly time-aligned physiological features. Document exclusions and negative controls. This is not a diagnosis study.

**Single active discovery blocker:** Find an accessible, independently adjudicated ANS dysfunction/progression reference cohort (cardiovagal, adrenergic and/or sudomotor). If unavailable under budget, report the block and identify an ethical/authorized clinical collaboration path rather than pretending healthy data are ground truth.

**Do not initiate another score, novel-sounding formula, gadget or product plan merely because an agent suggests one.**

## Approval requirement

Only the founder can authorize a mission pivot, after a brief documented comparison of: current mission, proposed mission, evidence, scientific opportunity, lost work, financial cost, and reason the current track fails. *Agent enthusiasm and market criticism are not approval.*

## Permanent progress-report format

Report these five lines in every meaningful research review:

1. **North star:** ANS dysfunction / deterioration marker discovery
2. **Current gate:** G1–G6 status and concrete blocker
3. **What was proven:** Exact tested method / dataset / population, not aspirational impact
4. **What was NOT proven:** Especially novelty, clinical validity and outcome progression
5. **Next falsifiable step:** One experiment only, with a stop condition

This record is deliberately independent of an agent's opinions and should not be overwritten to manufacture success.
