# ANS-001 — Scientific novelty reset and go/no-go decision

**Date:** 2026-11-15
**Status:** Critical gap identified. We have no verified original autonomic biomarker or unique clinical use claim. The current program has produced measurement infrastructure, reproducibility work and a proposed predictive experiment; these are valuable but **not a discovery**.

## Primary prior-art findings (verified at publisher / PubMed)

1. **Age clock on our exact PhysioNet source:** Schumann et al. (2023), *Front Aging Neurosci*, PMID 36755773. Used resting ECG/BP and 29 autonomic indices to predict chronological age in 884 adults. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36755773/
2. **More recent cardiovascular autonomic age gap:** Schumann et al. (2025), *Am J Physiol Heart Circ Physiol*, PMID 41138205. Age model and cardiovascular-risk association in 1,060 healthy adults; it is an age-risk association, not longitudinal survival prediction. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41138205/
3. **Heart-rate fragmentation is already a prognosis biomarker:** Costa et al. (2018), *Front Physiol*, DOI 10.3389/fphys.2018.01117 tested incident cardiovascular events. Further published MESA reports address cognitive decline, incident AF, and low-coronary-calcification risk (PMID 40471909). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40471909/
4. **Active stand recovery already predicts long-term outcomes:** *Responses to active stand test predict 12-year incident cardiovascular disease and mortality* (published 2025), *Communications Medicine*, PubMed PMID 41390547, showed full standing hemodynamic response patterns and recovery associated with 12-year events and death after covariate adjustment. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41390547/ and https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-025-01253-3
5. **Dynamic standing phenotypes and frailty:** *Peripheral autonomic dysregulation in response to standing: a new form associated with cardiovascular disease, mobility reduction, and cognitive alterations* (2026), PMID 41614653 describes reproducible cardiac-output orthostatic response subgroups with clinical associations. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41614653/
6. **Motor-autonomic coupling and frailty prior work:** A 2024 study tested cross-system coupling using upper extremity function and cardiac-autonomic measures, so coupling two systems is **not automatically novel**. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11128670/

## Correction to previous framing

- The independent review's assertion that the 12-year TILDA stand analysis was 'only a preprint' is obsolete: a **published 2025 analysis exists**. Do not advertise challenge-based or standing-recovery prognosis as unstudied.
- Framing original HRF/MUSIC outcome replication as biomarker *discovery* was an overstatement. It is defensible as **external replication and transportability**.
- The research team must not claim originality for PIP/W1, BRS, an age-gap score, active stand HRR10–20, orthostatic BP recovery, or ordinary feature combinations.

## Candidate white-space hypotheses — all unverified, NOT claimed to be new

**A. Within-person autonomic perturbation-to-recovery *trajectory***: Instead of estimating age or health from one resting session, quantify each person's **change from their own reproducible baseline** after standardized benign challenge, including recovery kinetics, asymmetry and variation across repeat visits. Test whether *within-person deterioration* predicts future functional decline beyond established HRF, BRS, HRV, stand recovery and clinical risks. Must screen serial/autonomic digital-twin and repeated-challenge literature first.

**B. Recovery-system discordance / cross-mechanism residual**: Test whether individuals with conventional resting HRV but abnormal BP stabilization, respiratory-cardiac coupling or cerebral perfusion after challenge are at elevated future risk. Explicitly compare against published active-stand full-trajectory PCA/functional scores and motor-cardio coupling (2024). A composite becomes novel only if definitions, mechanism and independent incremental prognosis withstand prior-art screening.

**C. Population-context-adjusted response trajectories**: Compare *verified* pregnancy/perimenopause/athlete/heart disease subgroups using life-stage-specific baselines and repeat testing, to detect dysfunction rather than physiological adaptation. Requires measured phenotype, medication and exposure labels. Population-specific normalization and personalized recovery scores are not assumed novel without literature and patent review.

## Required novelty test before expanding implementation

For each hypothesis:
1. Search PubMed/Google Scholar/ClinicalTrials.gov/patent databases for direct overlap, using precise equations, task timing, features, population and outcome—not a branded label.
2. Build a claim chart: exact prior work, same data and signal, method, outcome, incremental baseline, missing element, and feasible falsifier.
3. Use a longitudinal dataset with standardized *repeated* challenge signals + clinically meaningful future outcomes. If none is accessible, **classify as untestable at current budget**, not breakthrough.
4. In a preregistered protocol, include state-of-the-art published **full active-stand functional-trajectory** baseline, resting HR and HRV, HRF, established risk factors; no weak straw-man comparator.
5. Require external cohort validation, measurement repeatability and uncertainty-bound benefit. Reject underpowered or post-hoc apparent improvements.
6. Obtain expert input before patentability, diagnostic or treatment claims; this quick scientific review is **not a freedom-to-operate opinion**.

## Continue, pivot, stop

- **CONTINUE** HRF/MUSIC only as pipeline clinical replication benchmark, not our proposed unique invention.
- **PIVOT R&D** toward experimentally falsifiable *within-person longitudinal recovery dynamics* IF prior art and access review reveal a real gap.
- **STOP claims** of a never-done-before biomarker until those checks are complete.
- **KILL commercial claim** if the candidate adds no meaningful, externally reproducible prediction over state-of-the-art approaches.

**Bottom line:** Potential originality must be *demonstrated through a gap in prior methods and a novel, validated result*, not inferred from new branding or combining standard measurements.
